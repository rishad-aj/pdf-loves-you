FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before dropping your first file.

Is PDF Loves You really free?

Yes — completely. There are no accounts, subscriptions, credits, watermarks or download limits, and there are no ads anywhere. Every tool is available to everyone.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The page uses your browser's own PDF engine (PDF.js and pdf-lib) to read and write files locally. There is no document server, no storage bucket and no analytics on file contents. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads.

Are there limits or watermarks?

There are no artificial limits. Output files are clean: no watermark, no footer branding, no “created with” page. The only practical limit is your device's memory.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All PDF, image and Office conversion engines are bundled with the app. One optional feature — OCR — downloads its recognition engine and language data the first time it is used, then caches it.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Yes, if you know the password. Use Unlock PDF to create a copy without the password, then edit it with any other tool. To add a password, use Protect PDF, which writes AES-256 encryption entirely in your browser.

Why does a converted Word or Excel file look different?

PDF is a fixed-layout format; Word and Excel are flow-layout formats. PDF Loves You reconstructs text, lines and tables from the PDF's positioning data, which works well for text documents but cannot perfectly recreate complex magazine layouts. Scanned documents need OCR first.

Is this affiliated with iLovePDF or Adobe?

No. PDF Loves You is an independent, original build inspired by the convenience of modern PDF suites. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to iLovePDF, Adobe, or any other brand. All code, copy and artwork here are original.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The interface is fully responsive and touch-friendly, including the signature pad and page editors. Very large documents will be slower on mobile than on a desktop, since all processing happens on your device.
